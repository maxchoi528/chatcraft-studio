Why standard browser copy-paste breaks in Microsoft Word

When you highlight a ChatGPT, Claude, or DeepSeek response and press Ctrl+C, clipboard HTML pulls dark background rectangles, literal pipe characters (| col |), and all of your conversational questions mixed in between sections.

How ChatCraft's in-browser OpenXML compiler fixes it

  • Native Word Tables & Headings: Compiles real OpenXML w:tbl structures with dark header shading, borders, and tabular numbers.
  • Executive Report Mode (Hide Prompts): Automatically strips every user prompt block so only polished AI sections merge into a deliverable report.
